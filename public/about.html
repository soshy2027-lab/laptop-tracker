About Laptop Tracker

🚀 Our Mission

Laptop Tracker was created to help people in East Africa protect their valuable devices. We know how important laptops are for students, businesses, and professionals. Our goal is to provide an affordable, easy-to-use tool that gives you peace of mind.

💡 How It Works

When your laptop connects to the internet, our system records its IP address and estimates its location. Even if the laptop is stolen, as long as it connects to the internet, you can see where it last was!

We also provide an "Auto-Tracker" script that runs in the background to update your location every 5 minutes automatically.
